# Real City Visual Rules

For full 3D asset art direction, `docs/visual-style-guide.md` is the primary source. This file is a compact conversion layer for building briefs.

Real City uses an original setting: Эолия, a bright fantasy world rebuilding after the Great Silence. The world is not empty because it was never inhabited; it is empty because old cities, roads, workshops, markets, towers, gardens, factories, and river settlements were abandoned. Players arrive as new city founders and compete to rebuild, claim ruins, exploit resources, and defend territory.

## Mood

- Reclaimed world, not apocalypse horror.
- Practical settlers and builders, not fairy-tale miniatures.
- Bright strategic readability, not dark cinematic realism.
- Gradual movement from earth/wood/stone to brick/metal/glass/concrete.
- Soft magic exists through eolian crystals and old ruins, but should not dominate every object.
- Optimized stylized mid-poly, not primitive low-poly: final buildings need shaped silhouettes, visible bevels, volumetric facade elements, readable props, and hand-painted-like material treatment.

## Palette

Use broad material contrast:

- Earth: warm brown, compacted soil, exposed clay.
- Grass and plants: fresh green with occasional windleaf shapes.
- Wood: pale cut wood, darker bark, simple planks.
- Stone: light gray and weathered blocks.
- Brick: muted red-orange, not overly saturated.
- Metal: dark gray, iron, steel highlights.
- Glass: pale blue-green, late-game only.
- Concrete: light gray, clean massing for modern stages.
- Oil/fuel: black oil pools, dark tanks, amber fuel lights.
- Eolian crystals: thin elongated amethyst hexagonal crystals, premium/magical accent only.

Avoid one-note palettes. A settlement should read as a mixed-material growing city.

## Shape Language by Category

Housing:

- Starts low and earthy.
- Moves to wood, then stone.
- Later splits into dense vertical city housing and prestige villas/palaces.
- Needs domestic props: doors, windows, chimneys, fences, small yards, lights.
- Avoid `cube + roof + flat door` briefs; even early housing needs a designed entrance, roof thickness, foundation, props, and material breakup.

Fields and beds:

- Low, tile-readable stages of plant growth.
- Super-versions should be visibly special: size, glow, unusual color, rare product detail.

Industry:

- Strong functional silhouettes: chimneys, tanks, belts, stacks, loading areas.
- Mid/late stages may require fuel systems: pipes, fuel barrels, darker industrial parts.

Defense:

- Walls evolve by material: wooden, stone, concrete, modern.
- Each stage has internal levels and must show reinforcement, not just recolor.
- Towers should be clear landmarks but should not hide too much map information.

Roads:

- Ground roads are low and rough.
- Improved roads are cleaner and more structured.
- Paved roads/masonry need brick-heavy material readability.

Ruins:

- Capturable map objects with buffs.
- Should look ancient, useful, and partially reclaimed by nature.
- Preserve a clear interactable silhouette.

## Upgrade Continuity

When an object upgrades, keep at least one recognizable anchor:

- same footprint edge;
- same doorway location;
- same chimney/tower axis;
- same foundation stones;
- same color accent;
- same road connection point.

This helps the player understand that the same object evolved rather than being replaced by unrelated art.
